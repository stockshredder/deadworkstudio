Loadout

Tracks production gear, cases, and equipment so nothing gets lost or left behind. Using a phone, video and photos are uploaded to check list items.

Follow us — Instagram (opens in a new tab) · YouTube (opens in a new tab) · TikTok (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Gear gets packed in a hurry and unpacked in an even bigger one. A missing case doesn't show up as missing until it's needed on set.

What it does

Loadout checks gear in and out against a list, using phone photos and video to confirm each case and item before it leaves — or before it's needed.
