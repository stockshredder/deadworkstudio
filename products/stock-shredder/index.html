The problem

Terabytes of footage from finished jobs never make it to a stock marketplace, because sorting, trimming, and tagging it by hand costs more time than it's worth.

What it does

Stock Shredder reviews raw footage, selects and trims the usable shots, exports them, and writes the keywords — ready for Artgrid, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Pond5, and Storyblocks.
