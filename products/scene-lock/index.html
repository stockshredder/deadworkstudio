Scene Lock

Organizes pre-production with storyboards, shot lists, camera plans, references, and schedules. Provides iPhone capability so everyone can see the shots with a clock to see where production is.

Follow us — Instagram (opens in a new tab) · YouTube (opens in a new tab) · TikTok (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Shot lists live in one app, storyboards in another, and the schedule in a group text. By the time everyone's looking at the same plan, half the shoot day is gone.

What it does

Scene Lock keeps storyboards, shot lists, camera plans, references, and the schedule in one place — with an iPhone view so anyone on set can see the shot and the clock without asking.
